## [Motor Learning and Transfer Are Symmetric Across Hands](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.17.739258v1.abstract)

_I. Nyamsuren, A. Statham, E. Mitchell, **B. Kohler**, P. Lam, R. L. Klatzky, and J. S. Tsay_ &mdash; _bioRxiv preprint, July 2026_

Across a meta-analysis of 114 datasets and preregistered experiments with 526 participants, we find that sensorimotor adaptation and interlimb transfer are strikingly symmetric across hands. The results suggest a fundamental dissociation: motor control is lateralized to the dominant hemisphere, while motor learning is supported by a symmetric neural architecture.
