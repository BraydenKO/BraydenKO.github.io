# [Yttri Lab](https://labs.bio.cmu.edu/yttri/)

## [B-REAL: A keypoint-less behavior platform for offline classification and real-time detection](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.13.758637v1)

_A. Eagle, **B. Kohler**, V. Kamrowski, N. Lingam, R. Li, and E. A. Yttri_ &mdash; _bioRxiv preprint, October 2026_

B-REAL is a pose-free behavior classifier that maps raw video to behavior faster than video frames are recorded, even on a CPU. It matches or outperforms established methods with substantially less training data and enables real-time, closed-loop behavioral experiments with minimal annotation and standard hardware.

# [Physical Intelligence Lab](https://www.tsaylab.com/)

## [Motor Learning and Transfer Are Symmetric Across Hands](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.17.739258v1.abstract)

_I. Nyamsuren, A. Statham, E. Mitchell, **B. Kohler**, P. Lam, R. L. Klatzky, and J. S. Tsay_ &mdash; _bioRxiv preprint, July 2026_

Across a meta-analysis of 114 datasets and preregistered experiments with 526 participants, we find that sensorimotor adaptation and interlimb transfer are strikingly symmetric across hands. The results suggest a fundamental dissociation: motor control is lateralized to the dominant hemisphere, while motor learning is supported by a symmetric neural architecture.
